## Invariants (all slices)

- Do not change lock/open rules, the priority order (DESIGN §5), the allowlist, or what children can write.
- No SQL in any of the three slices.
- The server decides and the client only shows. Durations use server time; the play plan is the only client-only state.
- Feelings are shown **only to the child**:
  - The comic and the feeling lines use child-session routes only.
  - A parent gets 403 from those routes. No parent page shows them.
- No points, streaks or rankings. An empty day is never scolded: copy is "không sao", "—", or nothing.
- Buttons are ≥ 44 px. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- UI text in Vietnamese; code, comments and commits in English.

## Slice R6d — Hôm nay: 4 mảng đủ ý (artifact `vHome`, item I)

Changes `PillarCards` in `app/today/tabs.tsx`. The four cards stay a 2-column grid (`sm:grid-cols-2`), headed "1 · Cảm xúc", "2 · Học tập", "3 · Kỹ năng", "4 · Vui chơi". Each card has a small tag on the right.

### 1 · Cảm xúc

Shown only when Góc bình yên is on. Tag: "Góc bình yên · chỉ con thấy".

- **Visited today:** a soft row with the word's emoji (`wordEmoji(word)`), "Hôm nay con đã ghé: **{label}**", and " · {activity title} ✓" when an activity was done. Below it, a link "Vào Góc bình yên lần nữa" to `/binh-yen`.
  - This needs today's `activity` key. **R6d adds** `activity: string | null` to `WeekFeel`, read from `by_checkins.activity_key` (`WeekCheckinRow` gains `activity_key`, and the server query selects it). R5 reuses it.
- **Not visited:** "Trước khi học, trong lòng con thế nào? Chạm một vùng ở thẻ trên." plus a link "Vào Góc bình yên".
  - The 4 region tiles stay in the top invite card (R6a). They are not repeated here.

### 2 · Học tập

Tag: "{done}/{total} việc".

- A progress bar for part 1: done ÷ total, filled in the child's colour, `role="progressbar"` with values.
- **"Việc tiếp theo" row** for the next task:
  - The next task is the same one Hôm nay already suggests: `nextFromPlan(s, view.plan) ?? nextTask(s)`, limited to part-1 items.
  - The row shows an eyebrow "Việc tiếp theo · {group}", the title, and `{sub/whereLine} · {due label}`.
  - The button reads "Bắt đầu" (todo) or "Tiếp tục" (running), and posts `/api/tasks/start` exactly like `NextTask` does.
  - For a running task, "Tiếp tục" opens the focus view (`ctx.onFocus`).
  - For a homework task that needs proof and is running, the row shows "Nộp ảnh bài" (`ctx.onProofOpen`).
- When part 1 is complete, the row shows "Xong hết bài hôm nay ✓".
- A ghost button "📚 Vào Góc học tập · xếp bài" calls `onStudy`.

### 3 · Kỹ năng

Tag: "làm song song".

- One row per part-2 item:
  - Done: `{title}` with "ít nhất N phút" and "✓ Xong".
  - Not done: `{title}` with "ít nhất N phút · làm lúc nào cũng được" and a "Bắt đầu" / "Tiếp tục" button (same actions as above).
- No skills today: "Hôm nay không có kỹ năng."

### 4 · Vui chơi

Tag: "Giờ tự do {freeMinutes}'".

- **Locked:** "Còn {n} việc nữa là có {freeMinutes} phút của con. Chọn trước con sẽ làm gì:".
  - `n` = part-1 + part-2 items that are not done, not counting upcoming items.
  - Below it, chips: `role="group"`, `aria-label="Giờ tự do con định làm gì"`, `aria-pressed`. Tapping a chip again clears it.
- **Running:** a notice "Giờ tự do đang chạy · {mm:ss}" with "Máy mở mọi trang." and "Con định: {plan}" if one was chosen.
- **Used:** "Đã dùng giờ tự do hôm nay (từ {hh:mm})." plus the plan line if any.
- **Chip lists** (`lib/today/playIdeas.ts`, pure, by voice):
  - Detective (Nguyên): "Đọc truyện tranh", "Vẽ / cosplay", "Nghe nhạc", "Ra ngoài".
  - Scientist (An): "Thí nghiệm nhỏ", "Ngắm sao", "Chơi game", "Đạp xe".
- **Storage:** `lib/today/playPlan.ts`, client only.
  - localStorage key `play-plans` holds a JSON object `{ "YYYY-MM-DD": "choice" }`, keeping the last 31 dates.
  - `readPlayPlan(date)`, `writePlayPlan(date, choice | null)`, every access wrapped in try/catch.
  - Read it after mount (`useEffect`), not during render, so the server-rendered page matches.
  - The comic (R5) reads the same key for the Giờ tự do panel.

### Tests

- Vitest:
  - `playIdeas` returns 4 per voice.
  - `playPlan` reads and writes, trims to 31 dates, and survives broken JSON or a missing `window`.
  - Pure helper `leftCount(s)`.
  - `buildWeekDays` reads `activity`.
- e2e (extend the R2/R6a Hôm nay test):
  - The 4 cards are present.
  - The Học tập card shows a progressbar and a "Việc tiếp theo" button that starts a task (status running).
  - A Vui chơi chip toggles `aria-pressed`, and the choice persists after reload.

## Slice R6c — Góc học tập (artifact `vStudy` / `workPanel`, items K, L)

Changes `StudyCorner` in `app/today/tabs.tsx` (extract to `app/today/study-corner.tsx` if it grows past ~300 lines) and `Breadcrumb` in `app/child-nav.tsx`.

1. **Drag to reorder (⋮⋮).**
   - Each not-done row gets a grip "⋮⋮" (aria-hidden) and can be dragged with mouse, touch or pen using pointer events, not HTML5 drag (HTML5 drag does not work on iPad).

2. **Row look.**

     - Bài trên lớp: the child's colour.
     - Bài bổ sung: `#6A706B`.
     - Kỹ năng: `#4C70A8`.
     - Làm trước ★: `#A87A0E`.
   - Title, plus a sub line: `{note/whereLine} · {due label}`, and "≥ N phút" for timed items.
   - The selected row gets a 2 px border in the child's colour on a soft background. Done rows are at 60 % opacity with ✓.
   - Each row has one main button that selects it (replaces the separate "xem chi tiết" pill).
3. **Header line:** "còn {n} việc" (same count as R6d).

4. **Focus mode.**

   - In focus mode the list is hidden and the work panel is centred, max width 640 px.
5. **Work panel** (keep `NextTask` logic; adjust only what is listed):
   - Eyebrow "Đang làm / Việc tiếp theo · {group}", title at 26 px, and the sub line.
   - **Big timer:**
     - Timed items show the remaining minimum as mm:ss from `canFinishAt`, ticking with `ctx.nowMs`.
     - Running homework shows elapsed time.
     - Not running shows "00:00".
     - The timer uses `aria-live="off"`.
   - Under the timer, the explanation:
     - Homework: "Bài trên lớp không có thời gian tối thiểu. Làm xong trong vở thì chụp ảnh nộp."
     - Timed items: "Ít nhất N phút. Nút Xong mở khi đủ giờ (giờ máy chủ)."
   - Tools row:
     - "Mở {appLabel} ↗" when there is a deep link.
     - "📎 Xem đề bài" when the item has attachments. It opens the existing `FileLinks` list, collapsed by default.
6. **"← Quay lại" (L).**
   - `Breadcrumb` gets an optional `onBack`. It renders a "← Quay lại" button (≥ 44 px) before "Con đang ở: …".
   - **Today screen:** on any tab other than Hôm nay, Quay lại goes to the previous tab the child came from: a tab history stack kept in `TodayScreen` state, falling back to Hôm nay. On Hôm nay it is not shown.
   - **`/binh-yen`:** Quay lại uses `router.back()` when `window.history.length > 1`, else `/today`.
   - **`/nhan-vat`:** keeps its existing back link; no change.

## Slice R5 — Truyện của Nguyên (artifact `worldPanel` detective branch, `mangaPage`, `mangaCover`, `mangaReader`, item M)

Nguyên has the detective voice (`voiceFor(grade) === "detective"`). The comic is built **only from keys already stored**:
- day_sessions milestones;
- `by_checkins` quad, word and activity;
- the skill title of today;
- the play plan on this device.

No story text is written per day. There is no new table.

### Data (server, child only)

- `WeekFeel.activity` already exists (added in R6d).
- `lib/today/weekDaysServer.ts`:
  - `weekDays(ctx, weekStart?)` accepts a Monday.
    - Allowed range: from the Monday of the child's first record (first `day_sessions` or `by_checkins` row) to this week.
    - Anything else → `null`, and the route answers 404.
    - A bad date format → 400.
  - The answer adds `volume: number`, the volume number of that week: whole weeks since the Monday of the first record, plus 1. With no record yet, it is volume 1 for this week.
  - The answer adds `shelf: { weekStart, volume }[]`: past weeks with at least one record, newest first, at most 12. Computed with one query per table over the date range.
- `app/api/week/days/route.ts`: `GET /api/week/days?week=YYYY-MM-DD`.
  - Child session only (as today); a parent gets 403.
  - `no-store`.
  - Without `week`: this week (as today).

### Pure comic model: `lib/comic/model.ts` (shared server/browser, unit-tested)

- `LINE[quad]` holds 2 bubble lines each:
  - red: "Khoan… chưa kết luận vội!", "Hừm. Hồ sơ này nóng quá."
  - yellow: "Hôm nay đáng thành một tập truyện!", "Manh mối tốt đây rồi!"
  - blue: "Pin yếu rồi… nghỉ chút đã.", "Hôm nay hơi nặng nề."
  - green: "Yên quá. Ý tưởng đang tới.", "Một ngày dịu dàng."
- `SFX`: red "GRRR!", yellow "YAY!", blue "hừm…", green "~ phù ~".
- `TONE`: red `#F6DED8`, yellow `#F7ECCB`, blue `#DCE5F3`, green `#DCEDE0`.
- `comicPage(day, index, ctx)` returns the 5 panels:
  - **A · day panel.**
    - With a feeling: the quad, `LINE[quad][index % 2]`, `SFX[quad]`, the face expression (`QUAD_EXPRESSION[quad]`), and the box "Hồ sơ {đỏ/vàng/lam/lục} · **{label}**".
    - Speed lines for red and yellow.
    - Without a feeling: an empty panel "Khung mở đầu: ghé Góc bình yên để vẽ". When Góc bình yên is off, the empty panel shows the date only.
  - **B · "Cách xử lý".**
    - With an activity: its emoji (`activityEmoji`) and its detective-voice title.
    - With a feeling but no activity: 📝 "Gọi tên là đủ cho hôm nay".
    - Otherwise: empty "chưa vẽ".
  - **C · "Nhiệm vụ học".** When study is done: 📚, SFX "XONG!", box "Bài hôm nay ✓". Otherwise empty: "đang làm…" today, "hôm nay chưa xong — không sao" on a past day.
  - **D · "Kỹ năng".** When skill is done: 📖, box with the skill title (today: from DayState part-2 titles, otherwise "Kỹ năng ✓"). Otherwise empty: "đang làm…" today, "nghỉ" on a past day.
  - **E · "Giờ tự do".** When play is done: bubble "Yeah!", smile face, box = the play plan for that date from localStorage, else "{freeMinutes} phút tự do". Otherwise empty: "xong hết là có" today, "—" on a past day.
  - Future days in the current week are not pages.
- `comicCover(week)`:
  - "TẬP {volume}", the calm face, "Thám tử {avatar.name || first name}", and "Tuần dd – dd/mm" with the child's icon.
  - A table of contents: one button per day page, showing the weekday, the word emoji and the word (or "đang vẽ" today).

### UI: `app/today/comic.tsx` (client)

Faces use `Character` (R3a/R3b: drawn face or Picrew picture).

- **`ComicToday`** — on Hôm nay, for the detective voice, in the same place as An's `WorldMini`.
  - Card "Trang truyện hôm nay", with "Xem Tập của tuần →" (switches to tab `tuan`) and "Mỗi mảng con làm xong là một khung được vẽ thêm."
  - 4 panels, 4 columns (2 on narrow screens): KHUNG 1 CẢM XÚC, KHUNG 2 HỌC TẬP, KHUNG 3 KỸ NĂNG, KHUNG 4 GIỜ TỰ DO.
  - Filled panels follow the artifact `.panel` styles; empty panels have a dashed border and a hint ("Ghé Góc bình yên", "Xong bài trên lớp", "Xong kỹ năng", "Có {freeMinutes} phút của con").
  - A panel that became filled since the last render pops in (`.pop`).
  - Data: `week` (already fetched on Hôm nay) plus DayState.
- **`ComicVolume`** — tab 4 for the detective voice.
  - Title "📕 Tập {volume} · tuần này" and "Chủ nhật tập được đóng lại, lên kệ truyện".
  - A reader showing one page at a time: the cover, then each day Monday → today.
  - A pager with "← Trang trước", dots (Bìa, T2 … CN, `aria-current`, ≥ 44 px) and "Trang sau →".
  - A shelf "Kệ truyện" with spines "Tập N" for past weeks. A spine loads `/api/week/days?week=…` and shows that volume read-only, with a "← Về tập tuần này" button.
  - Below: the existing "4 mảng của con trong tuần" card (`WeekAreas`) and `WeekProgress`, unchanged.
  - The page grid follows `.mgrid`: panel A spans 2 columns; then B | C; then D | E.
  - Screen dots, speed lines and SFX are CSS/SVG and decorative (`aria-hidden`). Each page is an `<article aria-label="Trang {weekday dd/mm}">`.
- **Tab label:** `tabLabel("tuan", world)` becomes voice-aware:
  - scientist → "Thế giới";
  - detective → "Tập của tuần" (icon 📕 in `ChildNav`).
  - The breadcrumb and nav use it.
  - `ChildNav` gets `voice` instead of a boolean, or a second flag. Callers: `today-screen.tsx`, `binh-yen-screen.tsx`, `nhan-vat`.
- **Góc bình yên "Xong rồi" (detective)**:
  - `POST /api/by/checkins` returns `comic: { quad, word, label, activity }` for detective, built from the saved check-in.
  - The corner shows a mini panel "KHUNG 1 · CẢM XÚC" (bubble = label, face with that expression, activity line) and "Khung 1 đã được vẽ vào trang truyện hôm nay.", with a link "Xem Tập của tuần →" (`/today?tab=tuan`).
  - Nothing is sent for scientist (they get `world`).

### Edge cases

- Week with no data: the cover and pages show empty panels with the calm copy.
- Sunday: the current week is still "tuần này" until Monday 00:00 VN, then it moves to the shelf.
- A Picrew picture that does not load: `Character` falls back to the drawn face.
- A parent opening `/api/week/days?week=…`: 403. A scientist child: the route still works (the week strip uses it), but no comic UI is shown.
