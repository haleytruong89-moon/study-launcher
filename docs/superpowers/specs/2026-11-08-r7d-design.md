**Rules for the second half (pure-knowledge cards).**
- Mostly `quads: []` (suits any check-in); the ≥ 2-non-held-cards-per-quad rule still applies to every topic that has cards.
- Still sourced (links in `scripts/field-notes-doc.ts`, checked date), hedged where the science is uncertain, and nothing scary or sad (same forbidden-word list; no predation, injury, death, extinction doom, disasters told as frightening).
- **Must not repeat facts already in An's world packs** `lib/world/{rung,bien,vu_tru,nui_lua}.ts` (she sees those through her world plant).
- Body 2–4 sentences, ≤ 380 characters; keys stable forever; tests in `tests/fieldNotes.test.ts` turn the four `it.todo` items into count checks automatically once a topic has cards.

**Fit to the check-in.** Each card lists the quads it suits (`quads`). Red/blue cards calm and reassure; yellow/green cards are about wonder and curiosity. Every topic must have **at least 2 non-held cards per quad**. Topics with only 4 cards will therefore need several cards marked "any" (`quads: []`).

**Safety for an 11-year-old** (on top of Nguyên's rules):
- Nothing scary or sad about animals: no predation, injury, death, extinction doom or animal testing details. If a source used captive animals, the card must not describe distress.
- No clinical words: "rối loạn", "bệnh", "triệu chứng", "điều trị", "chẩn đoán", "ảo giác", "hỏng".
- No self-harm, eating, weight or body-image content.
- No "con nên / con phải" and no comparison with other children.
- Claims are hedged and every card is sourced. Prefer peer-reviewed papers by DOI, or a reputable body: Smithsonian, National Geographic, NOAA, NASA, BBC Earth/Science Focus, Greater Good, APA, NIH.
- **Try-its** stay gentle and safe: breathing, noticing, drawing, writing one line, looking outside, a short walk.

**Data shape.** Reuse Nguyên's engine instead of copying it:
- **New file `lib/binhYen/fieldNotes.ts`:**
  - `ScienceTopic` = the 9 keys above;
  - `SCIENCE_TOPIC_KEYS` (chip order as in the table), `SCIENCE_TOPICS` (label + emoji), `SCIENCE_TOPIC_TARGET` (counts), `FIELD_NOTES: readonly FieldNote[]`, `activeScienceTopics()` (topics with shown cards, chip order);
  - `FieldNote` has the same fields as `Dossier` (`key`, `topic`, `quads`, `title`, `body`, `tryIt?`, `source`, `held?`, `heldReason?`).
- **Generalise the pure selection in `lib/binhYen/dossier.ts`.** Make `pickDossier` / `fitsQuad` generic over `{ topic; quads; held? }`, keeping the same rule and `dayIndex`. Then add `fieldNoteFor(date, quad, topic)` (null only for a topic with no cards yet) and `findShownFieldNote(key)`. Nguyên's behaviour must not change; her tests must still pass untouched, apart from type-only edits.
- **Parent reading copy:** `docs/binh-yen/SO_TAY_KHOA_HOC.md`, generated by `scripts/field-notes-doc.ts` in the same way as `scripts/dossier-doc.ts`, with per-card source links in the script. It has the same sections as `HO_SO_MAT.md`: purpose, safety rules, how cards are shown, every card in full, a sources table, and a held section if any card is held.
- **Tests (`tests/fieldNotes.test.ts`):**
  - every topic that has cards has its target count (first half: 40 cards), a pending `it.todo` for each empty topic, unique keys;
  - body 2–4 sentences and ≤ 380 characters;
  - every card has a source and valid quads;
  - the forbidden-word regex (Nguyên's list, plus "chết", "giết", "săn mồi", "tuyệt chủng", "đáng sợ");
  - at least 2 non-held cards per quad for every topic that has cards;
  - deterministic selection;
  - every card string appears in the doc.
